Privacy Policy

Homey Fan is a personal script used only by its owner, on the owner's own Homey hub, to control the owner's own Nest thermostat.

What it accesses

Through the Google Nest Smart Device Management API it reads the thermostat's fan, heating/cooling and Eco status, and it sets the fan timer.

Where data goes

The Google sign-in tokens and the readings stay on the owner's Homey hub. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at nestservices.google.com/partnerconnections or in your Google Account's third-party access settings.
